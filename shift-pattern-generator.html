A rotation looks simple on paper: work 2, off 2, work 3. The hard part is laying it over a real calendar to see which weekends you work, how many teams a post needs and how much overtime payroll will count. This generator does that for 11 preset rotations or a cycle you type in.

How to use the shift pattern generator

  1. Pick a rotation. Choose one from the Rotation list. For the fixed 2-2-3 (Pitman), fixed 4-on-4-off, 4-3 and 5-on-2-off patterns, use These shifts are to switch between day and night shifts. For Custom pattern, type one letter per day in Custom cycle: D day, E evening, N night, X 24-hour, O off.
  2. Set day 1. In Day 1 of the cycle, enter any date your team starts the pattern. Team A starts on that date, and Show the calendar for switches to the other teams, which run the same cycle from different points so their workdays interlock.
  3. Adjust the details. Open Shift times and calendar settings to change each shift's start time and length, show 1 to 12 months from any starting month, and choose the day your weeks and payroll start on.
  4. Read the results. You'll see average hours per week, cycle length, longest run on, longest break, people per 24/7 post and federal overtime for your payroll week.
  5. Check coverage and overtime. Below the calendar, the coverage grid lines up every team day by day, and the overtime table compares all seven payroll start days.
  6. Take it with you. Add to my calendar (.ics) saves every shift in the months shown as events for Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook. You can also download a CSV, print the calendar or copy a share link.

How the generator does the math

  • Average hours per week: the hours of every shift in one cycle, divided by the cycle length in days, times 7. A 2-2-3 cycle has seven 12-hour shifts in 14 days: 84 ÷ 14 × 7 = 42.
  • Longest run on and longest break: counted across the point where the cycle repeats, so a run that carries over into the next cycle is counted in full.
  • People per 24/7 post: 168 hours in a week ÷ average weekly hours. That's 4 at 42 hours, 3 at 56 hours and 4.2 at 40 hours. It assumes nobody is ever absent; the staffing calculator adds vacation and sick time.
  • Federal overtime: the hours over 40 in each 7-day workweek, starting on the day you chose, averaged over enough weeks for the cycle and the calendar to line up again. Each shift counts in the workweek where it starts.
  • Coverage: the grid shows each team's first 14 to 28 days and notes whether every shift is covered exactly once a day.

Example: one 2-2-3 team, two payroll weeks

Your day team's cycle starts on a Monday. You work Monday, Tuesday and Friday through Sunday, then Wednesday and Thursday the next week: 42 hours a week on average. With payroll weeks running Sunday to Saturday, your weeks alternate 48 and 36 hours, for 8 overtime hours every two weeks, or 4 a week. Start the payroll week on Monday and the same shifts fall into 60- and 24-hour weeks: 20 overtime hours every two weeks, or 10 a week. Nothing changed except where the week begins.

Choosing a pattern

Start with what you need covered. For a post staffed 24/7, Panama, DuPont, rotating 4-on-4-off and continental each use four teams to cover every shift exactly once a day; 24/48 and 48/96 do the same with three platoons on 24-hour shifts. The 4-3, 5-on-2-off and 9/80 presets are standard and compressed workweeks, not round-the-clock rotations.

Fixed patterns cover only one shift. The fixed 2-2-3 and fixed 4-on-4-off presets show two teams that share the day shifts, or the night shifts, between them. For 24/7 coverage you need a second pair of teams on the other shift, four teams in all. Names vary: some schedulers use Pitman for fixed day and night teams and call the rotating version Panama, which is how this tool labels them.

Then weigh fatigue. The Canadian Centre for Occupational Health and Safety recommends rotating forward from day to afternoon to night, allowing at least 24 hours off after each set of night shifts and avoiding start times as early as 5 or 6 a.m. The continental preset rotates forward and starts mornings at 07:00 by default; you can change all three start times under Shift times and calendar settings. Our rotating shift patterns guide compares the trade-offs of each pattern.

Why the payroll week changes your overtime

Under the federal Fair Labor Standards Act, each workweek stands alone: hours can't be averaged across two or more weeks. The workweek is a fixed, recurring block of 168 hours that can begin on any day and at any hour. Once it's set, it stays put, and an employer can change it only if the change is meant to be permanent and isn't designed to avoid overtime.

So a 42-hour average doesn't simply mean 2 overtime hours a week. Across this tool's 42-hour presets, the average runs from 2 to 12 hours a week, depending on the pattern and how its cycle lines up with the payroll week. Check the overtime table before you set up payroll, not after.

Two limits to know. The tool's workweeks begin at midnight on the day you pick, so it shows a 9/80 as alternating 44- and 36-hour weeks. Because a workweek can begin at any hour, an employer can start it halfway through the 8-hour day so both weeks total 40. And the tool applies only the federal 40-hour weekly rule. Public fire departments can use work periods of up to 28 days under section 7(k), with overtime after 212 hours in 28 days, and state rules can differ. See our overtime laws guide.

Questions people ask

How many teams do I need to cover a post 24/7 with 12-hour shifts?

Four. One post needs 168 hours of coverage a week, and four teams averaging 42 hours each add up to exactly 168. The generator shows this as people per 24/7 post: 168 divided by the pattern's average weekly hours.

Why does my overtime change when I change the payroll week start day?

US federal overtime is counted one workweek at a time, and hours can't be averaged across weeks. Moving the start day regroups the same shifts, so a 2-2-3 day team can go from alternating 36- and 48-hour weeks to 24- and 60-hour weeks.

Can I put my rotation in Google Calendar or on my phone?

Yes. Add to my calendar (.ics) downloads every shift in the months you're viewing as events that Google Calendar, Apple Calendar and Outlook can open. To cover more time, set up to 12 months under Shift times and calendar settings first.

How do I enter a rotation that isn't in the list?

Choose Custom pattern and type one letter per day of the cycle: D for day, E for evening, N for night, X for a 24-hour shift and O for a day off. For example, DDNNOOOO is two days, two nights and four days off. Then set each shift's start time and length.

Does the overtime figure include state overtime rules?

No. It applies only the federal rule of overtime after 40 hours in a workweek. State laws, section 7(k) work periods for public fire and police employees, and rules outside the US can differ, so check the rules that apply to you.
