The Shift Hack Challenge

Share the trick that gets you through nights, rotations or 12-hour shifts. It's free to enter, the best entries are judged on usefulness, and the top three win cash.

Contest 1 is open

Theme: your best shift-work hack. A sleep trick after nights, a meal-prep system for 12s, a commute routine, a way to keep family time on a rotation, a smarter way to swap shifts. If it makes shift life easier and others can use it, enter it.

Entries close November 30, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time. Winners are announced here on December 10, 2026.

  • First place$100
  • Second place$50
  • Third place$25

The top ten entries are featured on 824824.com and in The 8/24 Brief, with your permission.

Enter now

How it works

  1. Enter by November 30Describe your hack in 50 to 1,500 characters. Add a link to a photo or short video if it helps.
  2. Eligibility check, December 1 to 3We confirm entries follow the rules and are original.
  3. Judging, December 1 to 7Judges, including at least one working shift worker, score every eligible entry.
  4. Winners announced December 10Winners are emailed first and have 7 days to reply.
  5. Prizes paid within 30 daysAfter a winner's identity and eligibility are verified.

How entries are judged

Usefulness: would other shift workers actually use it?40%
Originality: is it a fresh idea or a new twist?30%
Clarity: could someone follow it tonight?30%

Eligibility in brief: age of majority where you live, one entry per person, your own original work, and the contest is void where prohibited. No purchase or donation necessary. Read the Official Rules.

Questions people ask

Does it cost anything to enter?

No. No purchase, donation or subscription is needed, and none of those improves your chances.

Who can enter?

Anyone who has reached the age of majority where they live, except where the contest is prohibited. One entry per person. See the Official Rules for the full list.

How are winners chosen?

This is a contest of skill, not a draw. Judges score each eligible entry on usefulness (40%), originality (30%) and clarity (30%).

How are prizes paid?

By PayPal, or as a gift card of equal value where PayPal isn't available, within 30 days after the winner is verified.
