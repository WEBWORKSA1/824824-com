Questions people ask

Do you publish rates?

We share a rate card on request. Founding partners who book before our first anniversary keep their launch pricing for 12 months.

Can a sponsor change a calculator's results or a guide's advice?

No. Sponsors get labeled placement and credit, never editorial control. Results, rankings and advice stay independent.

What won't you advertise?

Payday or predatory lending, gambling, multi-level marketing, and anything that misleads workers about their pay or legal rights.
