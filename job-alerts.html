Questions people ask

How much do job alerts cost?

Nothing. Alerts are free for job seekers, and every email has an unsubscribe link.

Will employers see my details?

Only if you tick the box that lets recruiters and employers contact you. Otherwise we only use your details to send alerts.

Which areas do you cover?

Alerts are rolling out region by region. Sign up now and we'll confirm by email when matching alerts start in your area.

Can I change what I get?

Yes. Reply to any alert or use the contact page to change your role, area, shifts or pay range, or to stop alerts.
