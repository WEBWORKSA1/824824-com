Free tools for shift work

Eleven calculators for pay, time, schedules and sleep. They all run in your browser, work on your phone and need no account.

Pay and overtime

  • Time card calculatorWeekly or bi-weekly time cards with breaks, overnight shifts, rounding, overtime and pay.
  • Overtime calculatorOvertime pay with the correct regular rate when differentials or bonuses apply.
  • Shift differential calculatorEvening, night and weekend premiums, flat or percentage, per shift and per year.
  • Hourly and salary converterHourly, weekly, bi-weekly, monthly and annual pay, including 12-hour rotations.

Time math

  • Hours calculatorHours between two times or dates, add or subtract time, and add up a list of shifts.
  • Decimal hours converterHours and minutes to decimal hours for payroll, and back again.
  • Military time converter24-hour and 12-hour time, how to say it out loud, and a full chart.
  • Printable timesheetA clean weekly or bi-weekly timesheet to fill in by hand and print.

Schedules and staffing

  • Shift pattern generatorTurn 2-2-3, DuPont, Panama, 4-on-4-off, 24/48 and more into a calendar you can export.
  • 24/7 staffing calculatorHow many people it takes to cover every hour, with absences and overtime built in.

Sleep and health

  • Night shift sleep plannerA sleep, nap, light and caffeine plan built around your exact shift.
