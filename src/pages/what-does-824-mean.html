There's no single, ancient meaning of 824. Instead, the number picks up different readings depending on the language, tradition or calendar you look at it through. Here's a tour, with sources, and the reason this site took the name.

The short answer: for us, 824 means 8 of 24, eight hours of the 24-hour day. Numerology and wordplay readings below are cultural traditions, shared for interest, not claims of fact.

In Chinese and Cantonese

8: prosperity

In Mandarin (bā) and Cantonese (baat), 8 sounds like the word for prospering or getting rich, which is why it's prized in phone numbers, prices and dates.

4: avoided

4 (sì) sounds like the word for death. Some buildings skip fourth floors, a habit known as tetraphobia that is also common in Korea and Japan.

2: easy, paired

2 is usually seen as a good number, and in Cantonese it can sound like the word for easy.

24 versus 28

In Cantonese, 24 can sound like “easy to die,” while 28 sounds like “easy prosperity.” So 824 reads as mixed luck, and there's no established traditional reading of 824 or 824824.

In Korean number wordplay

Korean speakers have long turned numbers into words, especially in the pager era of the 1990s. The best known is 8282, read as ppalli ppalli, “hurry, hurry,” according to The Korea Times. Businesses still chase phone numbers that spell something. 824 has no established pun of its own.

In Japanese wordplay (goroawase)

Japanese goroawase reads digits as syllables. August 24 (8/24) is registered as Toothbrush Day, because 8-2-4 can be read as ha-bu-rashi, toothbrush, according to bestcalendar.jp. Commemorative days like this are registered by companies with Japan's anniversary association, and 8/24 carries several of them.

In numerology

Vedic

The digits are linked to Saturn (8), the Moon (2) and Rahu (4). Reduced, 8 + 2 + 4 = 14, and 1 + 4 = 5, the number of Mercury.

Chaldean

The Chaldean system popularized by Cheiro links 4 to Uranus instead of Rahu. The doubled number 824824 sums to 28, then 10, then 1, the number of the Sun, as Satyori summarizes the system.

Angel number 824

In modern Western spiritual folklore, 8 stands for abundance, 2 for balance and partnership, and 4 for hard work, read together as steady effort bringing reward, as Astrology.com puts it.

A reminder

Numerology is a cultural and spiritual tradition. It isn't science, and nothing on this page is a prediction.

In scripture: verses numbered 8:24

Several sacred texts have a verse 8:24, often searched alongside the number:

  • Bhagavad Gita 8.24 describes the path of light, fire and the sun's northern course taken by those who know Brahman (Vedabase).
  • Romans 8:24 speaks of being saved in hope, and that hope that is seen is not hope (Bible Gateway).
  • Matthew 8:24 tells of a storm that swamps the boat while Jesus sleeps.
  • Quran 8:24 (Al-Anfal) calls believers to respond to what gives them life (Quran.com).

In work and the economy: 8 of 24

This is the meaning we built on. In 1817, Robert Owen argued for a day split into eight hours of labor, eight of recreation and eight of rest. Stonemasons in Melbourne won an eight-hour day in 1856, US workers struck for it on May 1, 1886, Ford moved to eight-hour shifts in 1914, and the International Labour Organization's first convention, in 1919, set a limit of 8 hours a day and 48 a week (overview). Our history of the 8-hour day tells the full story.

Today, a large share of those eight-hour blocks fall outside the daytime. In the US, 15% of wage and salary workers don't work a regular daytime schedule, according to the Bureau of Labor Statistics. Scheduling them is big business: one market research firm, Global Market Insights, estimated workforce-management software at $8.8 billion in 2023.

On the map and in the law

India: PIN 824

Postal codes beginning 824 cover parts of Bihar, including Aurangabad and Gaya districts. Bodh Gaya, where the Mahabodhi Temple marks the place of the Buddha's enlightenment, is 824231 (Maps of India).

United States: ZIP 824

ZIP codes 82401 to 82450 serve towns in Wyoming's Bighorn Basin, including Worland, Powell and Cody, the town named for Buffalo Bill near Yellowstone's east entrance (Cody).

Phone numbers

824 is not currently assigned as a North American area code; it's listed as available for future use (area code list).

US law

Section 824 of Title 16 of the US Code is part of the Federal Power Act. It puts the interstate transmission and wholesale sale of electricity under federal regulation (16 U.S.C. § 824).

August 24 in history

  • 79: the traditional date of the eruption of Mount Vesuvius that buried Pompeii, though some evidence points to an autumn date.
  • 1572: the St. Bartholomew's Day massacre began in Paris on the night of August 23 to 24.
  • 1814: British troops burned the White House and the Capitol during the War of 1812.
  • 1991: Ukraine declared independence; August 24 is its Independence Day.
  • 1995: Windows 95 went on sale.
  • 2006: the International Astronomical Union reclassified Pluto as a dwarf planet.
  • 2016: Los Angeles declared August 24 Kobe Bryant Day, after the two jersey numbers he wore, 8 and 24 (FOX 11).

Born on August 24

The writers Jorge Luis Borges (1899) and Paulo Coelho (1947), the composer Jean-Michel Jarre (1948), the writer and actor Stephen Fry (1957), the baseball player Cal Ripken Jr. (1960), the actor Marlee Matlin (1965), the comedian Dave Chappelle (1973) and the actor Rupert Grint (1988) were all born on August 24 (list).
