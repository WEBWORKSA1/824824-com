Questions people ask

Are the tools really free?

Yes. Every calculator works without an account. The site is paid for by advertising, optional referral fees when businesses ask us for software recommendations, sponsorships and reader support.

Do you see the hours and pay I enter?

No. Calculations run in your browser, and your entries are saved only on your device so you can come back to them. We only receive what you choose to send through a form.

Can I rely on the overtime results?

Use them as a careful estimate. Presets follow published rules for common places, but exemptions, averaging agreements and collective agreements can change the answer. Check your contract and the official sources we link.

Why is the site called 824824?

It reads as 8 of 24: the eight-hour share of the day, twice. See what 824 means across cultures.
