Open roles

Writers with shift-work experience

Nurses, care aides, paramedics, security, plant and warehouse workers: write practical guides from the inside. Paid per piece.

Editors and fact-checkers

Check every law, threshold and statistic against official sources. Employment-standards or payroll knowledge is a plus.

Front-end developer

Plain JavaScript calculators that must be correct to the minute, accessible and fast on old phones.

Designer

Interfaces, illustrations and printable templates that tired eyes can read at 3 a.m.

Video creators

Short explainers and tool walkthroughs for YouTube and social channels.

Community moderators

Help run contests, review entries and keep discussions useful and kind.

Contest judges

Working shift workers who help score the monthly Shift Hack Challenge.

Commission-based. Connect us with scheduling vendors, staffing firms, unions and employers.
