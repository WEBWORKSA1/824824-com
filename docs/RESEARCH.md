# 824824.com: research, concept decision and revenue model

Prepared October 8, 2026. Sources are linked inline; anything not verified is marked as an assumption.

## 1. Decision in one paragraph

Build **824824.com as "Eight of Twenty-Four": the free toolkit for shift work**. 824824 reads naturally as "8 of 24", the eight-hour share of the day, so the name fits work-time tools. That cluster has six-figure monthly search demand and a multibillion-dollar B2B market behind it, and the trademark risk is low. AdSense monetizes the worker traffic. The profit engine is B2B: managers who use the staffing calculator, pattern generator and scheduling guides get routed into a consent-based "get matched with scheduling/payroll software" funnel. Sponsorships, featured jobs, contests and reader support round it out.

## 4. Revenue model (scenarios, not forecasts)

Every number below is an **assumption** to be replaced with your own AdSense, analytics and partner data after 30 to 60 days.

- **AdSense:** page RPM $5 to $12 for a US-heavy HR and pay audience.
- **B2B leads:** 25% of visits come from managers or schedulers; 0.5% to 1% of those finish the funnel; each lead earns $40 to $100 through vendor partner programs or direct cost-per-lead deals.
- **Sponsorships:** start once traffic passes about 50,000 pageviews a month; $250 to $1,500 per tool or newsletter slot a month.
- **Visits:** pageviews ÷ 1.6.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25,000 | $125–$300 | 20–40 | $800–$4,000 | $0 | **~$0.9K–$4.3K** |

What decides the outcome: lead revenue only exists once partner agreements are signed. Signing two or three scheduling, time-clock or payroll vendors before traffic arrives is the highest-leverage task after launch.

### Gaps no competitor fills well (built into 824824)

1. Overnight and 24-hour shifts handled correctly on the time card, with start-day versus split-at-midnight attribution.
2. A rotation-to-calendar generator with .ics export, team coverage and **overtime by payroll week start** (for example, a 2-2-3 rotation produces 4 or 10 overtime hours a week depending on the workweek start).
3. Overtime using the US **regular-rate** method when differentials and bonuses apply, showing the underpayment.
4. State and provincial presets: California (daily 8/12 and 7th day), Alaska/Nevada, Colorado, Canada federal, Ontario 44, Quebec 40, British Columbia, Alberta.
5. Rest-gap warnings (11 hours) between shifts.
6. A shift-work sleep planner (anchor sleep plus a nap, caffeine cut-off, light timing).
7. A 24/7 staffing calculator that feeds the B2B funnel with team size prefilled.
8. Offline use: a service worker keeps visited tools working without signal.

## 6. Trademark and copyright scan

- No brand, app or hotline uses "824824" in web search results. WIPO, EUIPO and CNIPA databases weren't checked; run a quick search before filing any mark.
- Avoid implying any link with Kobe Bryant, LLC. Its US filings include "8", "24", "2 8 24", "24/8/365", KB24 and the Mamba marks. Also avoid Nike, the Lakers, 824Vision LLC, and Japanese anniversary-day registrants.
- The site uses only original text, original graphics and embedded (not re-hosted) videos, and every page carries a trademark and copyright notice. The full disclosure is in `disclaimer.html#trademark`.

## 7. 90-day plan and KPIs

1. **Week 1:** activate FormSubmit; submit the sitemap; apply to AdSense; email two or three scheduling, time-clock and payroll vendors about partner or CPL terms.
2. **Weeks 2–6:** publish state and province overtime pages (California, Texas, New York, Ontario, BC, Alberta). Publish one pattern page per rotation (2-2-3, DuPont, Panama, 4-on-4-off, 24/48) with a prefilled generator link.
3. **Weeks 4–12:** seed nursing, PSW, security and plant-worker communities with the sleep planner and pattern generator. Run Contest 1 and recruit shift-worker writers.
4. **KPIs:** tool completions per visit, funnel start-to-submit rate (target over 25%), leads per 1,000 B2B visits, page RPM, newsletter signups per 1,000 visits, and backlinks from embeds and shares.
