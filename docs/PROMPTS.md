# Phase-wise build prompts: 824824.com

Run the phases in order. Paste the **global brief** at the top of every phase prompt. Each phase ends with "Done when" checks. These are the prompts this site was built from; reuse them to rebuild, extend or clone the approach for another numeric domain.

## Global brief (paste into every phase)

```
Project: 824824.com, "Eight of Twenty-Four". Free tools and sourced guides for shift workers
and the managers who schedule them. 824824 reads as "8 of 24": eight hours of the 24-hour day.

Hard requirements:
- Static site (HTML/CSS/vanilla JS) generated by build.py; hosted free on GitHub Pages
  (repo WEBWORKSA1/824824-com, published from gh-pages). Relative URLs only, so it works at
  /824824-com/ and later at the root of 824824.com.
- Every page starts with a full-width bar linking to https://web.works/contact with the text:
  "Contact, if you are interested in this website / domain name / Sponsorship / Advertisement / Partnership".
- Google AdSense publisher ca-pub-6620975821265271: loader script in <head> on content pages,
  google-adsense-account meta, ads.txt. No ads on the funnel, contact, support, legal or 404 pages.
  Labeled ad slots with reserved height, never above a tool's controls.
- One inbox for all contact and form submissions: [SITE INBOX]. It must never appear in page
  text, HTML, JS or repo files in plain form: store it XOR-obfuscated in assets/js/config.js,
  assemble it only at submit or click time, and show only an "Email us" link (href falls back
  to contact.html). Forms post JSON to FormSubmit's AJAX endpoint; support a formAlias override.
- Avoid trademark issues: original text and graphics only, no third-party logos or likenesses,
  embedded videos only; a trademark and copyright disclosure in the footer and disclaimer.html.
- Accessible (WCAG AA contrast, keyboard, focus, reduced motion), responsive from 360px,
  light and dark themes, fast (no frameworks, lazy ads and videos), privacy-respecting
  (calculator data stays in localStorage).
- Facts: every law, threshold and statistic cites an official or primary source. No invented
  testimonials, quotes, ratings or traffic numbers.
```

## Phase 0: research and concept

```
Research what 824 / 824824 / 8-24 / August 24 mean across Chinese, Korean and Japanese number
wordplay, Vedic and Chaldean numerology, angel numbers, scripture verses numbered 8:24, work
history (the 8-hour day), postal codes, area codes, law codes and notable dates and people.
Score 5 concepts on search demand, revenue per visit, defensibility, trademark risk and brand
fit; pick one. Run a trademark scan for 824824 and related marks. Then audit 25+ leading sites
in the chosen niche (calculators, SaaS vendors, lead marketplaces, donation, contest and
sponsorship pages) and list table-stakes features, UX patterns to copy, gaps to own, and an ad
layout. Output docs/RESEARCH.md with sources.
```
Done when: a concept is chosen with numbers, the 25+ site audit is complete, and every claim has a link.

## Phase 1: brand and design system

```
Design a distinctive identity grounded in shift work. The colors come from roster shift codes:
day (sky), evening (sodium amber), night (indigo), rest (teal), on cool paper and a night-ink
background. Type: Big Shoulders (Chicago industrial display, for headlines and result numerals)
plus Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (body, built for tired eyes, tabular figures). Signature element:
a draggable 24-hour dial where an amber 8-hour arc is the shift, with a suggested sleep arc and a
live "now" pointer. Write assets/css/style.css with tokens, light and dark themes, and components
(buttons, chips, forms, choice tiles, tabs, results, tables, callouts, calendars, timelines,
ad slots, lead band, footer, print styles).
```
Done when: tokens pass contrast checks in both themes, and no generic template tells remain (no all-caps eyebrows or identical card grids).

## Phase 2: generator and shared chrome

```
Write build.py: read src/pages and src/guides (YAML front matter + HTML), expand shortcodes
({% include ad.html %}, cta-inline, lead-band, alerts-band, tool-list, guide-list, video-grid,
newsletter, share, mail, icon), wrap pages in layouts (home, page, hub, tool, guide), and emit
canonical URLs, Open Graph, JSON-LD (Organization, WebSite, WebApplication, Article, FAQPage,
BreadcrumbList), sitemap.xml, robots.txt, ads.txt, site.webmanifest, a search index, sw.js and
404 base href. Chrome: partner bar, sticky header with a Tools mega menu, search dialog (/ or
Ctrl+K), theme toggle, Get matched CTA, footer with newsletter, link columns, hidden-inbox mail
link and the trademark notice.
```
Done when: `python3 build.py` builds every page and `tests/qa.py` reports no broken links, missing anchors, duplicate ids or inbox exposure.

## Phase 3: pay tools

```
Build, as pure functions with node unit tests plus UI bindings:
1) Time card: 1 week, 2 weeks or custom periods; per-day in/out/break; split shifts; overnight
   detection (equal times = 24h); rounding (5, 6, 10, 15 min); start-day or split-at-midnight
   attribution; overtime presets (US federal 40; California 8/12/weekly 40/7th day; Alaska and
   Nevada; Colorado 12; Canada federal 8/40; Ontario 44; Quebec 40; BC 8/12/40 counting the
   first 8 hours a day; Alberta the greater of 8/day or 44/week; custom); rest-gap, overlap and
   16-hour warnings; pay; print report with signature lines; CSV; share link; local save.
2) Overtime: US regular-rate method (straight-time pay including differentials and
   non-discretionary bonuses ÷ hours; premium = (multiplier − 1) × regular rate) versus a simple
   method, showing the underpayment.
3) Shift differential: evening and night windows (flat or %), whole-shift majority rule,
   weekend premium, per shift, week and year.
4) Hourly/salary converter including 36, 42 and 48-hour rotations and regular overtime.
```
Done when: the unit tests reproduce the worked examples ($20/h × 50 h with a $40 differential and a $50 bonus = $1,199 versus $1,190).

## Phase 6: lead generation

```
Build get-matched.html: a 5-step funnel (industry → team size, locations, operating pattern →
needs, must-haves, current tool → timeline, budget, role, country, postal code → contact). It
has a progress bar, saves answers locally, prefills from URL parameters (?industry=, ?team_size=,
?pattern=), and generates a buyer's checklist from the answers before asking for contact
details. Consents: a required "may contact me" box, an optional "share with up to three
providers", an optional newsletter box, and a referral-fee disclosure. Add job-alerts.html (role,
area, shifts, job type, minimum pay, separate recruiter and SMS opt-ins), a home lead band whose
industry tiles start the funnel, inline CTAs in guides, an aside CTA on tools, a newsletter in the
footer, and an exit-intent "Shift Survival Kit" (desktop only, once a week, delivered on-page).
```
Done when: a mocked submission carries every answer, the consent text and the page, and the success panel shows next steps.
