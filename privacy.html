Privacy policy

Short version: the calculators run on your device and we never see what you type into them. We only receive what you choose to send us through a form. Advertising partners such as Google may use cookies, and you can control that.

Last updated October 8, 2026.

Who we are

824824.com (“824824”, “we”) is an independent website offering free tools and guides for shift workers. You can reach us about anything in this policy through the contact page; choose “Privacy request” as the topic.

Calculators: stored only on your device

Our calculators run entirely in your browser. To let you pick up where you left off, they save your entries in your browser's local storage on your own device. That data isn't sent to us. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data. If you use a “Copy share link” button, your entries are encoded into the link itself, so anyone you send the link to can see them.

Information you send us

When you submit a form, such as a software shortlist request, job alert, contest entry, support pledge, advertising inquiry, job application, newsletter signup or message, we receive the details you enter. That can include your name, email address, phone number, company, location and your answers. We also record the page you were on, the time of submission and the consent boxes you ticked, so we can show what you agreed to.

Form submissions are delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-forwarding service, which processes them on our behalf. Emails you send us directly are handled by our email provider.

How we use your information

  • To respond to you and provide what you asked for, such as a software shortlist, job alerts or a reply.
  • To run contests: checking eligibility, judging, contacting winners and paying prizes.
  • To send The 8/24 Brief, only if you ask for it. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To share your software request with up to three matching providers, only if you tick the box asking us to. That sharing may result in a referral fee to us.
  • To let recruiters or employers contact you about matching jobs, only if you tick that box.
  • To keep the site secure, prevent spam and abuse, and meet legal obligations.

Where privacy laws such as the GDPR apply, we rely on your consent (which you can withdraw at any time), on taking steps you've asked for, on our legitimate interests in running and protecting the site, and on legal obligations.

Cookies, advertising and analytics

We show ads through Google AdSense. Google and other third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your earlier visits to this website and to other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads to you based on your visits to our site and other sites on the internet. Learn more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

If we turn on Google Analytics, it uses cookies to tell us which pages and tools are used most, in aggregate. In regions that require it, a consent message lets you choose before ad or analytics cookies are used.

Your ad choices

  • Turn off personalized ads from Google in Google's Ads Settings.
  • Opt out of personalized ads from many third-party vendors at aboutads.info, or in Europe at youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Ads may still appear, but they won't be personalized.

Videos, fonts and hosting

  • Videos use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) and don't load until you press play. Once you play a video, YouTube's own privacy policy applies.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser sends a request, including your IP address, to Google's servers.
  • Hosting is provided by GitHub Pages, which may log technical information such as IP addresses for security and operations.

Who we share information with

We share personal information only with service providers who help us run the site (form delivery, email, hosting, advertising and analytics), with software providers or recruiters when you've ticked a box asking us to, with payment providers if you choose to support us, and when the law requires it. We don't sell your information to data brokers.

How long we keep it

  • Messages, shortlist requests and advertising inquiries: up to 24 months.
  • Job alert and newsletter details: until you unsubscribe.
  • Contest entries: up to 24 months after the contest ends; winners' records as long as tax law requires.
  • Job applications: 12 months, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR and UK GDPR, Canada's PIPEDA and Quebec's Law 25, and US state laws such as California's, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to get a copy of it, to withdraw consent, to object to or restrict some uses, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. To make a request, use the contact page and choose “Privacy request.” We'll respond within the time the law requires and won't treat you differently for using your rights. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

824824 is meant for working adults and isn't directed to children under 16. We don't knowingly collect their information. Contest entrants must have reached the age of majority where they live.

International transfers and security

Our service providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States. We use reputable providers and limit what we collect. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please don't send sensitive information such as health details or government ID numbers through our forms.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page when our practices change and revise the date at the top. Significant changes will be highlighted on the site.
