Official Rules: The Shift Hack Challenge (Contest 1)

No purchase, payment, donation or subscription is necessary to enter or win, and none will improve your chances. This is a contest of skill. Void where prohibited.

1. Sponsor

The contest is run by the operator of 824824.com (the “Sponsor”). You can reach the Sponsor through the contact page. The Sponsor's mailing address is available on request.

2. Contest period

Entries are accepted from October 8, 2026 at 12:00 a.m. to November 30, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time (the “Contest Period”). The Sponsor's server time is the official clock. Eligibility review runs December 1 to 3, judging runs December 1 to 7, and winners are announced on or about December 10, 2026.

3. Eligibility

  • Open to individuals who have reached the age of majority where they live at the time of entry.
  • Not open to the Sponsor, its contractors and judges, the members of their immediate families or households, or anyone involved in running the contest.
  • Not open where the contest is prohibited or restricted by law, or to residents of countries or regions subject to comprehensive trade sanctions.
  • One entry per person. Multiple entries, entries made with multiple email addresses, or entries made by automated means are disqualified.

4. How to enter

Complete the entry form on the contest page during the Contest Period with your name, email, region, a title and a description of your shift-work hack between 50 and 1,500 characters. You may add a link to a photo or video you own. Your entry must:

  • be your own original work, in English or French;
  • not include anyone else's private information, image or voice without their permission;
  • not infringe any copyright, trademark or other right, and not be defamatory, obscene, dangerous or unlawful;
  • not promote a business, product or service you have a financial interest in.

Entries that break these rules may be disqualified at the Sponsor's discretion. Advice involving medication, alcohol, drugs or unsafe driving is not eligible.

5. Judging

Eligible entries are scored by a panel of at least two judges, including at least one person who works or has worked shifts, using these criteria:

  • Usefulness (40%): how practical the hack is for other shift workers.
  • Originality (30%): how fresh the idea or approach is.
  • Clarity (30%): how easy it is to understand and follow.

Ties are broken by the higher usefulness score, then by a vote of the judges. The judges' decisions are final on all matters relating to the contest.

6. Prizes

  • First place: US$100.
  • Second place: US$50.
  • Third place: US$25.

Total approximate retail value of all prizes: US$175. Prizes are paid by PayPal or, where PayPal isn't available, by a digital gift card of equal value chosen by the Sponsor. Prizes can't be transferred and can't be exchanged for anything else, except that the Sponsor may substitute a prize of equal or greater value. Winners are responsible for any taxes on their prize and may be asked to provide tax information required by law before payment.

7. Odds

Winners are chosen by skill-based judging, not chance. Your chance of winning depends on the number and quality of eligible entries received.

8. Winner notification and verification

Potential winners are notified by email within 10 days after judging ends. A potential winner must reply within 7 days and may be asked to confirm eligibility and sign a declaration and release. Residents of Canada must also correctly answer a time-limited mathematical skill-testing question, without assistance, before being confirmed as winners. If a potential winner can't be reached, doesn't reply in time, or is found ineligible, the prize may go to the next-highest-scoring eligible entry. Prizes are paid within 30 days after a winner is verified.

9. Your entry and our license

You keep the copyright in your entry. By entering, you give the Sponsor a non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide license, for three years from the end of the Contest Period, to publish, display and promote your entry on 824824.com, in its newsletter and on its social media channels, with credit to your first name and region if you agreed to be featured. You confirm that you have the right to grant this license.

10. Privacy

Information you provide is used to run the contest, contact entrants and winners, and pay prizes, as described in our Privacy policy. Winners' first names and regions may be published with their permission. A list of winners is available on request through the contact page.

11. General conditions

  • The Sponsor may cancel, suspend or change the contest if fraud, technical failure or any other cause beyond its control affects the contest, or if too few eligible entries are received, and will post any change on the contest page.
  • The Sponsor isn't responsible for lost, late, incomplete or misdirected entries, or for technical problems that prevent an entry from being received.
  • To the extent the law allows, entrants release the Sponsor and its contractors from liability for any loss or injury arising from taking part in the contest or receiving a prize. Nothing in these rules limits rights that can't be limited under the law where you live.
  • This contest isn't sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with, any social media platform or payment provider.
  • These rules are governed by the laws of the place where the Sponsor is established, without regard to conflict-of-law rules, and subject to any consumer protection law that applies where you live. A French version of these rules is available on request.
